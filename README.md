# The E-MTB Evolution

Three riding seasons (2024–2026) compared: what changed when David's acoustic mountain bike was stolen and he moved to e-MTBs, which also left him with the energy for the gym 3–5 times a week. Interactive charts let you switch between measures, compare 2026 with the 2024–25 acoustic average, count each season's climbing in Everests, and estimate climbing for a ride of a given length.

**View the page: https://deuber.github.io/e-mtb-evolution/**

Data: mountain bike season totals from Strava. 2022–23 are left out because they included road riding. The 2026 season is still in progress (totals from early November).
